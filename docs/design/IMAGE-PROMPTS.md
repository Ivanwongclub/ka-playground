## Usage Notes

1. **Yes, you can upload images** — every placeholder `img-placeholder-*` div in the HTML has a `data-img` attribute identifying which prompt to use. Replace the placeholder with `<img src="your-uploaded-url" style="width:100%;height:100%;object-fit:cover">`.

2. **Lovable implementation:** Store all images in Supabase Storage under:
   - `scheme-images/cards/` — Card posters (A)
   - `scheme-images/heroes/` — Detail page heroes (B)
   - `scheme-images/sc5-gallery/` — STEM on Car gallery (C)
   - `scheme-images/news/` — News banners (D)
   - `scheme-images/phase-icons/` — Phase illustrations (E)

3. **Fallback:** The HTML uses gradient placeholders with category colours — these look good even without images.

4. **All images should:**
   - Feature Asian children/teens (Hong Kong international school context)
   - Avoid pure white/black edges (works on dark and light themes)
   - Be free of text, logos, watermarks
   - Feel premium and aspirational, not generic stock
